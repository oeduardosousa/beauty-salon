# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro with TypeScript. Recommended because the site is mostly content (services, staff, gallery) that should be static and fast, while the booking flow needs small interactive islands. Chosen by the assistant at the user's request; no deploy target was specified.

## Users

Primary: potential employers and clients evaluating the developer's work. They open the site to judge how complete and well-built a salon website is, and to see a realistic booking product.

Secondary: prospective salon clients, in the sense that the demo should feel like a real salon site a visitor could use.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio piece: a fully featured beauty salon website that demonstrates front-end and booking-flow engineering. Success means a viewer can click through every salon feature (booking, services, staff, gallery) and come away convinced the developer can build a production-grade site of this kind.

## Positioning

A complete salon product in one codebase, rather than a single-page template. The differentiator is breadth and polish: every common salon feature is present and works together.

## Operating Context

Viewed on desktop and phone, typically during a portfolio review. Content is placeholder but should read as plausible salon content.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Required features:
- Online booking flow: choose service, staff member, date and time, then confirm.
- Services and pricing menu: categories, durations, prices.
- Staff profiles: stylists with specialties and availability.
- Gallery and reviews: before/after work and client testimonials, all placeholder.

Bookings are simulated in temporary browser storage. No database, backend, or Supabase. See BRIEF.md.

## Brand Commitments

Salon name: placeholder, fictional. The user has not provided a name, logo, or colors. No brand commitments exist yet. Visual direction is deliberately not set here and belongs to new-work.

## Evidence on Hand

None. The project folder was empty at init. All testimonials, prices, staff, and gallery images must be placeholders; do not present invented reviews or claims as real.

## Product Principles

- Show every salon feature working together, not a single landing page.
- Placeholder content must be clearly fictional and plausible.
- Build for a reviewer who clicks through everything, so the booking flow must be complete end to end.
