## Direction contract

THESIS: The salon's booking is a linear installer wizard. Each step is a dialog the visitor completes before the next one opens, so the flow looks and feels like the offer itself. It refuses the category default of a hero photo over a booking button and a row of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: System gray dialog chrome with hard beveled edges, a dithered sixteen-color bitmap banner running down the left edge, bold system sans headings, and one saturated accent on the progress fill and the default button. Square corners, 1px dark bevel lines, no gradients, no soft shadows.

STORY: The visitor understands the salon offers services with named stylists, and that they can book a real slot in four steps. They believe the booking is simple and uncluttered. They act by pressing Next through the wizard to confirm an appointment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A single centered dialog, fixed width, on a gray desktop. The left edge carries the dithered banner with the salon name in the banner. Right of the banner: the step title "Choose a service", a progress bar showing step 1 of 4 filled in discrete blocks, the service list as radio rows, and Back and Next buttons bottom-right with Next default-focused. The primary action is Next.
